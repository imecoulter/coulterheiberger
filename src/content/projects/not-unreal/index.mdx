Made with non-engine tools during design studies, Not Unreal collects the early
academic projects where the foundational habits came from: iteration, working to
a brief, giving and taking critique, and finishing to a deadline.

The work spans an elementary school taken from site plan to classroom interior,
a cantilevered apartment block, a speculative lunar collage, and chipboard study
models photographed under hard light.

They are grouped here because of what they have in common — none of them were
made in a game engine, and all of them are the reason the engine work later had
something to stand on.
