Built entirely in Unreal Engine, Habitat is a small interactive environment
scaled to a box turtle's perspective. A simple movement system lets a player
navigate it, which is what makes the scale legible — the space only reads as
large because you cross it slowly and low to the ground.

The design was informed by research into turtle behaviour rather than by taste.
Box turtles respond to bright orange, so the orange forms are clustered near the
spawn point and the rest of the field is left pale; the animal is drawn into the
environment by something it is already looking for.

The four plates from the pond camera are one form iterated in place — low,
tall, orange, white — held at a fixed viewpoint so the variable is the object
and nothing else.
