Built in Unreal Engine and Houdini, Cecret is a snowy alpine visualisation based
on the terrain around Cecret Lake in Utah. Public elevation and satellite data
drove a procedurally generated landscape, which was then layered with custom
masks for colour, soil, sediment, limestone, sandstone and gravel.

The work was in the blending: getting material transitions to read as believable
geology rather than as textures meeting, and composing the frame so attention
lands where it should.

The masks are included here because they are the actual project. The two camera
renders are what the terrain looks like; the seven maps are what it is made of.
