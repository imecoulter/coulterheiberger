Developed in Unreal Engine using custom architecture modelled in Revit and
Rhino, Residence One is an early visualisation project built on terrain from
Vail, Colorado. The landscape came from elevation data; the scene was assembled
with Unreal's native tools.

The emphasis was on composition, staging and render efficiency — producing an
environment flexible enough to iterate in rather than a single finished frame.

Three of the eight plates are lookdev passes: the same cameras with every
surface swapped to grey checker and calibration spheres dropped into frame.
They are here because they are how the finished images were arrived at.
