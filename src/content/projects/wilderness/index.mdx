Created in Unreal Engine, Rhino and Revit, Wilderness is a large, playable
post-apocalyptic landscape designed to be inhabited by several different
animals. The terrain was modelled in Rhino, converted into a heightmap, and
developed as a sequence of connected biomes a player crosses in turn.

An abandoned, overgrown warehouse modelled in Revit anchors the scene. It is
there to suggest a human past without narrating one — the building persists,
the plants and water are taking it back, and the resilience of the natural
world is the point being made.

The three white axonometrics decompose that warehouse: the walled floor plate,
the long-span truss roof, and the single repeating structural module the roof is
built from.
