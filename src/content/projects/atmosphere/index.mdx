Constructed in Unreal Engine 5, Atmosphere is a set of early visualisation
studies exploring emotional tone through real-time rendering. The work was
importing assets, staging scenes, and pushing light, shadow and materiality
until each space carried a mood rather than merely describing a room.

The two stair frames are the argument in miniature: the same geometry, the same
lighting, one pair of eyes added in the opening at the top.
